- The source supports a maximum of nine exact product identities.

- Same-product front, rear and detail images must remain grouped under one slug.

## Recommended gallery order

For each approved cluster:

1. full front hero
2. full rear
3. suspender or three-quarter construction view
4. front embroidery detail
5. side/leg/hem detail
6. additional B2B construction view only when useful

## Quality decision

- White-background images are suitable for product pages after web conversion and consistent cropping.
- Do not replace a stronger existing hero without exact design matching.
- Retain original aspect ratio and avoid upscaling low-resolution details beyond their useful display size.
- No material species, certification, MOQ, lead time or production-capacity claim may be inferred from the images.
