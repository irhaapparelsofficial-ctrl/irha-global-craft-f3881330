# Irha Apparels — Real Image Asset Map

_Last updated: 2026-07-12_

Purpose: keep real Drive product images mapped to the correct live website product before any upload or replacement. Real images are preferred, but no image should be added just because it exists.

## Working rules

1. Audit first, publish second.
2. Real product photography is preferred over AI only when it improves buyer trust and matches the exact product.
3. Do not bulk-upload a folder directly into the catalogue.
4. Do not replace a polished hero image with a weaker real photo.
5. Use real close-ups for leather texture, embroidery, stitching, buttons and craftsmanship proof.
6. Keep same-product image sets together. Do not mix different Lederhosen variants into one product gallery without confirmation.
7. Misfiled or unrelated images are excluded.
