# Lead Wave Command Center v1

## Scope
- Surface the latest active lead campaign on the Admin Dashboard.
- Show discovered, needs-review, high-confidence, contact-ready, top-score and CRM-imported counts.
- Show country coverage and progress against the campaign target.
- Provide a direct shortcut to the existing Lead Acquisition review interface.

## Safety
- Read-only dashboard summary.
- No candidate is verified, imported, emailed or contacted by this component.
- Existing owner-controlled verification, CRM import and outreach boundaries remain unchanged.

## Current Wave
`DACH-NL Bavarian Buyers — Wave 1` is populated from official company sources and remains in `needs_review` until owner review.
