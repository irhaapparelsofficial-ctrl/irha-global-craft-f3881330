## Safe website workflow

1. Keep one canonical slug per exact garment identity.
2. Use the strongest clean full-front image as hero.
3. Follow with rear, three-quarter/suspender and construction/detail views.
4. Do not mix visually similar but different embroidery designs in one gallery.
5. Do not create products from filenames or file counts.
6. Do not infer material species, certifications, MOQ, lead time or production capacity from imagery.
7. Quarantine wrong-category, old-brand, size-chart and weak presentation media.
