# Automation Guardrails

The Irha automation system may research, summarize, draft, schedule internal work and prepare creative briefs.

It may not execute the following without the exact required approval and connector evidence:

- Send a prospect email
- Publish a public social post
- Import a lead into CRM
- Publish an SEO page
- Edit an external business listing
- Quote or change price
- Promise MOQ, payment terms, production lead time, delivery or shipping
- Approve samples, QC exceptions, refunds, complaints or shipment claims

Drafts and plans must remain clearly labelled as drafts/plans. Connector identity checks must never be represented as publication success. Every external action must retain actor, approval, timestamp, rules version and external evidence.
