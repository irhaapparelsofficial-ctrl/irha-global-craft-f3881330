## Core deduplication rules confirmed

1. One exact garment identity equals one canonical product/gallery.
2. Front, rear, side, suspender, belt, pocket, hem and close-up images are not separate products.
3. Colour/design variants are separate only when buyer-visible construction or styling materially differs.
4. One-image variants should not automatically become thin SEO pages.
5. Product-family pages are preferred for hats, belts, socks and other highly similar accessory variants.
6. No material species, fibre composition, certification, MOQ, lead time or production capacity is inferred from imagery.
