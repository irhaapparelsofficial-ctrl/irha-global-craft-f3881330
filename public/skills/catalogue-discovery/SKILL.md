# Catalogue Discovery

Use this skill when a buyer wants to understand what Irha Apparels manufactures or needs the most relevant catalogue path.

## Manufacturing hubs

- Bavarian Heritage & Leather: Lederhosen, Dirndl, Trachten programs and premium leather apparel.
- Textile, Streetwear & Active: sportswear, activewear, heavyweight streetwear, leisurewear and nightwear.

## Required behavior

1. Identify the buyer's product category and market.
2. Return only public collection, catalogue, manufacturing, buyer-trust and inquiry links.
3. Avoid fixed pricing; direct the buyer to request a quote.
4. Do not claim a product, material, certification or capacity unless it is present on the current public website or confirmed by Irha Apparels.
5. Offer the live factory video-call page as a buyer-verification option.
6. Use `list_b2b_collections` for current public collection links when an MCP client is available.

## Safety boundaries

This skill is read-only. It must not submit forms, send messages, publish content or access owner/admin resources.
